## Section B: Short Answer & Derivations (20 Points)

### Solution 6: The Role of `map -> odom` (5 Points)
- **Explanation (3 Points):** Autonomous navigation requires that `odom -> base_link` remains continuous and smooth because real-time feedback motor controllers compute linear/angular velocities from dead-reckoning derivatives. If discrete SLAM loop-closure jumps were published directly onto `base_link`, the controller would experience an instantaneous spike in error, commanding dangerous infinite acceleration and tripping motor safety circuits.
- **Mechanism (2 Points):** Publishing corrections to `map -> odom` absorbs the discrete localization offset while keeping `odom -> base_link` continuous.

### Solution 7: Quaternion Normalisation (5 Points)
- **1. Why rejected (3 Points):** A valid rotation quaternion must have unit length $\|\mathbf{q}\| = \sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2+w^2} = 1.0$. The vector $[0, 0, 0, 0]$ has norm $0.0$, which violates $SO(3)$ geometry and cannot define a direction.
- **2. Identity Quaternion (2 Points):** $[0.0, 0.0, 0.0, 1.0]$.

### Solution 8: 2D Coordinate Transformation (10 Points)
- **1. ${}^{\text{odom}}\mathbf{T}_{\text{base}}$ (2.5 Points):**
  $${}^{\text{odom}}\mathbf{T}_{\text{base}} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos(90^\circ) & -\sin(90^\circ) & 3.0 \\ \sin(90^\circ) & \cos(90^\circ) & 2.0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -1 & 3.0 \\ 1 & 0 & 2.0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$$
- **2. ${}^{\text{base}}\mathbf{T}_{\text{laser}}$ (2.5 Points):**
  $${}^{\text{base}}\mathbf{T}_{\text{laser}} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0.4 \\ 0 & 1 & 0.0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$$
- **3. ${}^{\text{odom}}\mathbf{T}_{\text{laser}}$ (2.5 Points):**

## Section C: Practical & Troubleshooting (20 Points)

### Solution 9: Timestamp Drift (10 Points)
- **1. Cause (5 Points):** System clocks on Computer S and Computer P are desynchronized. If Computer P's clock is ahead of Computer S's clock, transform queries generated by P request timestamps in S's future.
- **2. Engineering Solutions (5 Points):**
  1. Set up an **NTP / Chrony daemon** with Computer S acting as local time server over ethernet.
  2. In ROS 2 callbacks, query TF using `msg.header.stamp` with a non-zero timeout (`timeout=Duration(seconds=0.1)`) rather than `node.get_clock().now()`.

### Solution 10: CLI Diagnostics (10 Points)
Exact 4-command diagnostic sequence:
1. `ros2 node list`: Verify if the SLAM/localization node (`slam_toolbox` or `amcl`) is running.
2. `ros2 topic list`: Check if `/tf` and `/tf_static` are actively advertised.
3. `ros2 run tf2_tools view_frames`: Inspect the rendered PDF tree to see if `map` is disconnected from `odom`.
4. `ros2 run tf2_ros tf2_echo map odom`: Confirm live numerical updates and transform delays.

## Section B: Linear Algebra & Calculus (20 Points)

### Solution 6: Skew-Symmetric Cross Product (10 Points)
$\boldsymbol{\omega} = [0, 0, 3]^T$, $\mathbf{r} = [2, 1, 0]^T$.
1. **Determinant Cross Product (3 Points):**
   $$\mathbf{v} = \det\begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{\hat{i}} & \mathbf{\hat{j}} & \mathbf{\hat{k}} \\ 0 & 0 & 3 \\ 2 & 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0(0) - 3(1) \\ -(0(0) - 3(2)) \\ 0(1) - 0(2) \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -3 \\ +6 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}\,\text{m/s}$$
2. **Matrix $[\boldsymbol{\omega}]_\times$ (2 Points):**
   $$[\boldsymbol{\omega}]_\times = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -3 & 0 \\ 3 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$
3. **Matrix Multiplication (3 Points):**
   $$\mathbf{v} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -3 & 0 \\ 3 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -3 \\ 6 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}\,\text{m/s}$$
4. **Orthogonality Check (2 Points):**
   $$\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{r} = (-3)(2) + (6)(1) + (0)(0) = -6 + 6 = 0$$

### Solution 7: Jacobian & Gradient Descent (10 Points)
$x(\theta) = \cos(\theta)$, $x_d = 0.50$, $\theta_0 = 30^\circ$ ($\pi/6\,\text{rad} \approx 0.5236$).
1. **Current position and error (2.5 Points):**
   $x(\theta_0) = \cos(30^\circ) = 0.8660\,\text{m}$.
   $e = x_d - x(\theta_0) = 0.50 - 0.8660 = -0.3660\,\text{m}$.
2. **Jacobian (2.5 Points):**
   $J = \frac{\partial x}{\partial \theta} = -\sin(\theta) \implies J(\theta_0) = -\sin(30^\circ) = -0.5000$.
3. **Gradient Descent Step (2.5 Points):**
   $$\theta_1 = \theta_0 + \alpha J^T e = 0.5236 + 0.4(-0.5000)(-0.3660) = 0.5236 + 0.4(0.1830) = 0.5236 + 0.0732 = 0.5968\,\text{rad} \approx 34.19^\circ$$
4. **Verification (2.5 Points):**
   New position $x(\theta_1) = \cos(34.19^\circ) \approx 0.8272\,\text{m}$.
   New error $|0.50 - 0.8272| = 0.3272 < 0.3660$. Error successfully decreased!

## Section C: Probability & Sensor Fusion (20 Points)

### Solution 8: 1D Kalman Sensor Fusion (10 Points)
1. **Variances (2 Points):**
   $\sigma_1^2 = (0.30)^2 = 0.09\,\text{m}^2$, $\sigma_2^2 = (0.10)^2 = 0.01\,\text{m}^2$.
2. **Kalman Gain $K$ (3 Points):**
   $$K = \frac{\sigma_1^2}{\sigma_1^2 + \sigma_2^2} = \frac{0.09}{0.09 + 0.01} = \frac{0.09}{0.10} = 0.90$$
3. **Fused Mean $\mu'$ (3 Points):**
   $$\mu' = \mu_1 + K(\mu_2 - \mu_1) = 5.00 + 0.90(5.30 - 5.00) = 5.00 + 0.90(0.30) = 5.27\,\text{m}$$
4. **Fused Standard Deviation $\sigma'$ (2 Points):**
   $$\sigma'^2 = (1 - K)\sigma_1^2 = (1 - 0.90)(0.09) = 0.10 \times 0.09 = 0.009\,\text{m}^2$$
   $$\sigma' = \sqrt{0.009} \approx 0.0949\,\text{m} = 9.49\,\text{cm}$$

3. **Eigenvalues & Eigenvectors (2.5 Points):**

## Section C: Velocity Kinematics & Singularities (20 Points)

### Solution 8: Planar Arm Singularity (10 Points)
1. **Determinant Derivation (4 Points):**
   $$\det(\mathbf{J}) = (-l_1 s_1 - l_2 s_{12})(l_2 c_{12}) - (-l_2 s_{12})(l_1 c_1 + l_2 c_{12})$$
   $$\det(\mathbf{J}) = l_1 l_2 (s_{12} c_1 - c_{12} s_1) = l_1 l_2 \sin((q_1+q_2) - q_1) = l_1 l_2 \sin(q_2)$$
2. **Singularity conditions (3 Points):**
   $\det(\mathbf{J}) = 0 \implies \sin(q_2) = 0 \implies q_2 = 0^\circ$ (fully extended) and $q_2 = 180^\circ$ (folded back).
3. **Manipulability at $\theta_2 = 90^\circ$ (3 Points):**
   $\det(\mathbf{J}) = (1.0)(1.0)\sin(90^\circ) = 1.0$.
   $w = \sqrt{\det(\mathbf{J}\mathbf{J}^T)} = |\det(\mathbf{J})| = 1.0000$.

### Solution 9: Numerical IK Step Calculation (10 Points)
$\mathbf{p} = [0.50, 0.50]^T$, $\mathbf{p}_d = [0.52, 0.49]^T$, $\mathbf{J} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$, $\mathbf{q}_0 = [0.50, 0.30]^T$.

   $$\mathbf{e} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.52 - 0.50 \\ 0.49 - 0.50 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} +0.02 \\ -0.01 \end{bmatrix}\,\text{m}$$

   $$\Delta \mathbf{q} = \mathbf{J}^{-1} \mathbf{e} = \begin{bmatrix} 1.0 & 0.0 \\ 0.0 & 0.5 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} +0.02 \\ -0.01 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} +0.020 \\ -0.005 \end{bmatrix}\,\text{rad}$$
